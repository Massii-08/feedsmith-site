Good fits

A bot is the right shape when your community wants information pushed to them in Discord rather than chased down on a website: availability and price alerts, scheduled digests, on-demand lookups, and status notifications, all sourced from public data and delivered where your members already are.

DACH fit: the bot's messages and the build conversation can run in German, French, or Italian, with Switzerland / GDPR considerations handled up front.

Scope & ground rules

  • Public sources only. The bot draws on public, logged-out pages, factual, non-PII data.
  • Respectful collection. Any data gathering honours robots.txt and applies sensible rate limits.
  • You operate and own the data. The bot and the data behind it run under your control and ownership.
  • Reliability, not circumvention. Everything is built around dependable access to data that's already public, nothing gated or private.
