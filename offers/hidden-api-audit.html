What you get

  • Analysis of one public site you specify: how its public, logged-out pages serve data and whether a stable, structured feed is realistic.
  • A 1-page feasibility report in plain language: data points available, realistic refresh cadence, expected reliability, and the recommended next step.
  • ~50 real sample rows delivered as CSV or JSON, drawn from the actual public source — concrete proof, not a mockup.
  • An honest reliability read: where the feed is solid, where it may be fragile, and what would make it break.
  • A clear go / no-go recommendation so you can decide with facts instead of guesswork.

Why start here

This is the low-risk way to begin. For a small fixed fee you find out whether a public source can realistically power your tool, dashboard, or workflow — before anyone writes a full scraper. The sample rows are yours to keep even if you don't move forward, so the audit pays for itself in clarity.

A strong fit if you're DACH-based: the audit, report, and follow-up can run in German, French, or Italian, with Switzerland / GDPR considerations flagged up front.

Scope & ground rules

  • Public sources only. We work with public, logged-out pages — factual, non-PII data.
  • Respectful collection. We honour robots.txt and apply sensible rate limits so the source is never strained.
  • You operate and own the data. The sample and any future feed run under your control and ownership.
  • Reliability, not circumvention. The focus is on dependable access to data that's already public — we don't pursue anything gated or private.
