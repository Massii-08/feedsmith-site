The problem

A one-off scraper is great, until the source site changes. Public sites redesign, rename fields, and restructure their pages all the time. When that happens, a static scraper quietly returns empty rows or wrong values, and you find out only when something downstream is already broken.

A Managed Data Feed closes that gap. The feed isn't just built once and handed off, it's watched, maintained, and fixed so it stays correct as the public source evolves.

What you get

  • Setup: a robust scraper for your public source plus scheduled delivery, daily, hourly, or whatever cadence fits, to your chosen destination (file drop, database, or simple API).
  • Monitoring: the feed is actively watched for breakage and drift. When the source changes and the data stops looking right, I catch it.
  • Fixes included: I fix it when the source changes. Maintenance to keep the feed accurate is part of the monthly service, not a surprise invoice.
  • Monthly check-in: a regular touchpoint to review the feed, adjust fields, and adapt as your needs evolve.
  • Clean, consistent output in your format (CSV / JSON / API), under your ownership.

Scope & ground rules

  • Public sources only. The feed draws from public, logged-out pages, factual, non-PII data.
  • Respectful collection. Scheduling honours robots.txt and applies sensible rate limits, so the source is never overloaded.
  • You operate and own the data. The feed runs under your control; the data it produces is yours.
  • Reliability, not circumvention. The whole service is about keeping already-public data dependable, never anything gated or private.
